# Premium Web Design

Create a luxury feeling high-end website, 

as if you were a senior website designer 

working with startups for a premium web 

design agency called Crawio.

I want the website to feature the following 

pages: Homepage, Portfolio page, About page, 

Contact Us page, and a Blog page with 4 blog 

articles already populated.

For the homepage I want it to include a hero 

section with a title, copy, and "Get Started" 

button centred, with a large image underneath 

showing a premium website mockup/UI.

I want the homepage to then feature a:

* Featured In section, showing logos of 

  publications and platforms like Clutch, 

  DesignRush, GoodFirms, Forbes, Awwwards

* Testimonials section with a scrolling 

  carousel of testimonials, each testimonial 

  should also have the result that the client 

  got eg: "300% more leads", "Revenue doubled"

* Portfolio Section:

  Heading: "Our Work"

  Subheading: "Websites that drive real results"

  Grid layout showing 6 portfolio cards

  Each card should have:

  - Full width project screenshot/image

  - Project name

  - Industry/Category eg: Restaurant, 

    E-Commerce, Real Estate, Healthcare

  - A "View Project" button that glows 

    on hover

  Below the grid add a CTA button:

  "See All Our Work" linking to Portfolio page

* How It Works section with 3 cards:

  "Share Your Vision", "We Design & Build", 

  "You Get Results". Each card should have 

  an image as well

* Features section with a grid of 4 cards 

  showing Crawio's services with icons:

  Premium Web Design, Custom Development,

  SEO & Growth, Ongoing Maintenance

* Mission Statement section which communicates 

  why Crawio was built and our mission to help 

  businesses grow online globally

* FREE 20 MINUTE CONSULTATION SECTION:

  A bold standalone section that says

  "Book Your Free 20-Min Strategy Call"

  Subtext: "We'll analyse your business and 

  tell you exactly what your website needs 

  to attract more clients"

  Big CTA button: "Book Free Call Now"

  Add trust points below button:

  - No commitment required

  - 100% Free, no hidden charges  

  - Get a custom website roadmap

  - Limited spots available

* FAQ section with relevant questions about 

  web design services, timeline, pricing, 

  process

* Footer with logo, links, WhatsApp button, 

  social media icons

PORTFOLIO PAGE:

- Full grid of all projects

- Filter buttons by category:

  Restaurant, E-Commerce, Real Estate,

  Healthcare, Fashion, Local Business

- Each project card shows:

  Screenshot, Project name, Category,

  Result achieved eg: "40% more bookings"

- View Live Site button on each card

ABOUT PAGE:

- Our Story section

- Who we are and why we started Crawio

- Our Values: Quality, Speed, Results

- Team section

- Stats: 50+ Projects, 30+ Happy Clients,

  3+ Years Experience, 10+ Countries Served

CONTACT US PAGE:

- Heading: "Let's Build Something Amazing"

- Simple contact form: Name, Email, 

  Business Name, Message

- WhatsApp button: "Chat on WhatsApp"

- Email display

- "We respond within 2 hours" trust line

- Embed a Calendly style booking widget 

  for free consultation call

BLOG PAGE:

- 4 blog articles already populated

- Topics:

  1. "5 Reasons Your Business Needs a 

     Website in 2026"

  2. "How a Good Website Can Double 

     Your Revenue"

  3. "Web Design Trends That Actually 

     Convert Clients"

  4. "Why Indian Businesses Are Going 

     Global With Better Websites"

- Each article has cover image, title, 

  date, read time, and full article content

TOP NAVIGATION:

- Logo on left

- Anchor links: Services, Portfolio, 

  How It Works, Testimonials, Blog, Contact

- "Book Free Call" CTA button on right

- Sticky navigation that blurs background 

  when scrolling

DESIGN STYLE:

- Background: #0A0A0A pure black

- Primary text: #FFFFFF white

- Accent color: #FF4500 orange-red

- Luxury dark premium feel

- Inter font, light font weight

- Tight letter spacing for headings

- Non-heading text at lower opacity

Use Phosphor icons with light icon weight.

ANIMATIONS:

- When page loads, content animates from 

  0 opacity to 100 opacity and moves up

- All sections animate with opacity and blur 

  as user scrolls down

BUTTON STYLE:

- Neumorphism 3D style buttons

- Glows orange-red #FF4500 on hover

CARD STYLE:

- Glassmorphic style

- Background blur

- Low opacity borders

- Premium feel

MOBILE/TABLET:

- From tablet breakpoint down, navigation 

  collapses to burger menu

- Full height tray menu animates in from 

  the right

- Animates out smoothly when closed

Make this look like a $10,000 agency website 

that attracts premium clients from US, UK 

and Canada.
